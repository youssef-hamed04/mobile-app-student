EduPlatform is a study app for university students. This policy describes what
the app collects, why, and what it never does. It was written against the app's
actual behaviour rather than from a template.

## What we collect

**Account details you give us.** Your full name, phone number, gender, and your
university, faculty, department and academic year. An email address if you
choose to add one. A profile photo if you choose to upload one. These identify
your account and decide which courses apply to your year of study.

**Your device.** When you sign in we record the device's platform, OS version,
model name, and an identifier generated by the app. An account is tied to one
device at a time. This exists to stop one paid account being shared across many
people — it is the mechanism that makes paid content viable, not an attempt to
track you. Changing device requires an administrator's approval.

**How far you got.** Which lessons you opened, your position in a video, how
much you watched, and whether a lesson counted as complete. This is what powers
"continue watching" and your progress, and it is how a teacher knows a course is
being used.

**Playback integrity signals.** While protected video or a purchased document is
on screen, the app reports whether the screen is being recorded or captured, and
records screenshot attempts. Only the fact that it happened is sent — never the
image, never the screen contents.

**Notifications.** If you allow them, a push token from Expo's notification
service so we can tell you about a new lesson or an announcement.

**Purchases inside the app.** Which library documents and course parts your
account holds, and your credit balance.

**Technical records.** Our servers log the IP address and app version of
requests, as any web service does, to operate the service and investigate abuse.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites.
- No contacts, no calendar, no precise or approximate location.
- No camera or microphone access — the app blocks both permissions outright.
- No files from your device. Choosing a profile photo uses the system photo
  picker, which hands over only the one image you pick.
- No screen contents, ever.

## Why we may use it

To run your account and sign you in; to show the right courses for your year; to
remember your progress; to deliver content you have paid for and keep it from
being copied; to send notifications you asked for; to answer your support
messages; and to keep the service secure.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Who else sees it

- **Our hosting and storage providers**, who run the servers and store the
  videos and documents on our behalf.
- **Expo's push notification service**, only if you enable notifications.
- **Your instructor and the platform's administrators**, who can see your
  enrolment and progress in the courses you joined.
- **Authorities**, only where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Your account data stays while your account exists. Progress and purchase records
are kept as long as your enrolment is valid. Security and access logs are kept
for a limited period and then deleted.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile and change your password in the app. Notifications can
be turned off in the app or in your phone's settings. To request a copy of your
data or ask us to delete your account, write to the address at the top of this
policy; we will confirm your identity first, since a deletion request from the
wrong person would cost you access you paid for.

## Children

The app is intended for university students. It is not directed at children
under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We will update this page when the app changes, and the date at the top will say
when. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
